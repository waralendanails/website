What I offer

The commissions.

Swatch Photography

Accurate, detailed photographs that show a polish exactly as it is — colour, finish, texture, and all the magic in between.

Nail Art Photography

From simple to detailed, I shoot nail art that looks as good in a photo as it does in person. Happy to work from your brief, or bring my own ideas.

UGC & Reels

Short-form video for Instagram and TikTok: some finishes — holo, multichrome, reflective — only make sense when they move. I also do nail art tutorials, which tend to do well for saves and shares.

Campaigns & Editorial

For launches and new collections, I can shoot a cohesive set of images that feel like they belong together. Good for brands who want more than just a swatch.

Creative Direction

Not sure where to start? I'm happy to lead on concept, mood, and styling — from the first conversation to the final image.
